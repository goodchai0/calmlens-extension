# Privacy policy for CalmLens

Last updated: November 7, 2026

CalmLens does not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

## What the extension does

- **Page changes:** CalmLens changes how pages look (fonts, spacing, tints, hiding some YouTube sections) directly in your browser. It does not read, copy or transmit page content.
- **Settings:** your choices, including the list of sites you paused, are saved with the browser's built-in `chrome.storage.sync`. If you are signed in to your browser, it may sync them between your own devices. The developer has no access to them.
- **Current site name:** the popup reads the address of the tab you have open, only to show and save the "Pause on this site" switch.

## What it does not do

- It does not collect browsing history.
- It does not record what you type.
- It does not use cookies.
- It does not send any data to third parties.

## Permissions

The extension runs on all websites so your reading settings apply wherever you read. All processing happens locally.
